01 / Profile

A civil engineer who coordinates models, turns them into drawings that get built, and builds the tools that make it faster.

About

Between the model and the site.

I'm a civil engineer with over four years in Virtual Design and Construction. My work connects BIM in Revit and Navisworks with project planning in 4D and 5D: coordinating disciplines before anything reaches site, producing shop drawings from the coordinated model, and linking model quantities to BOQ and cost.

When a task repeats every week, I turn it into a tool. My Revit and Navisworks add-ins let a team of modelers spend their time on engineering instead of clicks.
